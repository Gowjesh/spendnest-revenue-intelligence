Interface Choices & Deliberate Omissions

Design Architecture & Honest Ground Truth

Included Purpose-Built Manager UX

  • KPIs with Defined Denominators: Explicit breakdown (17/44 -> 18/44 text coverage; 11/30 stored connects vs 9 verified conversations).
  • Priority Exception Queue: Instant visibility into customer retention risks ([M04] Cedarwave) and defect escalations ([M07] Willowfield).
  • Activity by Person: Distinguishes call representatives from calendar meeting organizers (organizers are not assumed to be reps).
  • Drilldown Detail Drawer: Evidence quotes, limitations, action items, verbatim turns, and raw source JSON in one click.

Included Replay Studio

One-click verification panel allowing any reviewer to validate idempotency, duplicate prevention, and override persistence in seconds.

Rejected Decision Speculative Account Linking & Deal Size ARR

Alternative Considered: Automatically cluster records by company name matching (e.g. Cedarwave M04 with M13) and guess contract ARR from loose conversational statements (e.g. Asterloom M03 mention of "INR 10-15 lakh monthly spend").

Why Rejected: Per DATA_CONTRACT.md and DATA_DICTIONARY.md, there is no authoritative CRM deal table or verified company-ID join. Conversational mentions represent employee personal spending, not software subscription ARR.

Trade-off Accepted: We present transparent conversation signals and explicit uncertainty boundaries rather than inventing artificial pipeline figures that generate false managerial confidence.

Architectural Decisions & Future Evolutions

Architecture Deterministic State Engine

Choice: Next.js client-state engine backed by browser LocalStorage rather than requiring an external SQL database or authentication server.

Benefit: Instant zero-latency replay testing, fully reproducible locally and online without backend dependencies or API keys.

AI Usage Grounded Intelligence

Choice: Pre-generated, verifiable evidence passages and coaching insights grounded directly in the 18 adapted texts.

Safety: Zero hallucination of contract terms, deal stages, or customer commitments. When text is absent, records remain in clean intake state.
